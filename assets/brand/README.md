## Color and spacing

Midnight `#0B1220` · Light N `#F7F9FC` · Electric blue `#79A7FF` · Secondary text `#596779` · Cool paper `#F4F6FA`.

Keep the node and N together. Preserve the padding inside square exports and the symbol's proportions. The primary square is the website header, footer, and favicon identity. A light N also remains visible in the navy reader navigation. At small sizes, use the symbol without the research descriptor.

The cover uses a refinement of the selected decision graph illustration, cropped to its lower path. The dark gradient protects the central text while fading out before the round node, preserving its light and glass detail. On the 1512 × 256 design canvas, the title uses Public Sans ExtraBold at 82 px and the descriptor uses SemiBold at 28 px. The primary PNG is rendered at twice that resolution. The illustration is conceptual artwork and contains no research findings.

## LinkedIn upload

LinkedIn's recommended dimensions were checked 18 September 2026: [LinkedIn Page image specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309/image-specifications-for-your-linkedin-pages-and-career-pages?lang=en). The recommended company logo is 400 × 400 px, with a 268 × 268 px minimum. The recommended company cover is 1512 × 256 px. The primary cover PNG uses the same proportions at 3024 × 512 px; a standard-size copy is also included. All upload PNGs remain below 3 MB.

For a GitHub README, use the high-resolution cover and display it at the desired layout width (for example, 1000 px). Increasing the file resolution preserves sharper text on high-density screens; the actual type is also larger, rather than relying on more pixels alone.

LinkedIn can trim covers horizontally or vertically across devices. Review its upload preview and keep the central wordmark and descriptor visible. LinkedIn publishes no numerical safe-area or logo-padding requirement. Transparent logos display on white; use `linkedin-logo.png` to preserve the navy square.

These assets are for a company Page. A personal profile cover uses a different recommended size, 1584 × 396 px: [LinkedIn profile cover specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a568217).

These exports do not change a LinkedIn or GitHub account by themselves.
